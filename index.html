Our story

A little flour, a lot of heart.

Hearth & Grain began with one second-hand oven, a wooden workbench, and a belief that bread should be given the time it deserves.

Today, we still work by hand, keeping our batches small and our doors open to the neighborhood. Our recipes change with the seasons, but our approach remains the same: honest ingredients, patient fermentation, and food made to be shared.

“The best part of baking is not the loaf. It is the moment people gather around it.”

— Mira Sen, founder & head baker
